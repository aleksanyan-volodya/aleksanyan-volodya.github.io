Volodya Aleksanyan

M1 Mathematics & AI · Université Paris-Saclay · Intern at GeePS, CentraleSupélec

Hi, I’m Volodya, a first-year Master’s student in Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence at Université Paris-Saclay.

I am currently doing a research internship at GeePS, CentraleSupélec, working on physics-informed complex-valued neural networks (CVNNs) to solve direct and inverse problems in electromagnetic wave diffraction, under the supervision of Marc Lambert.

Before the Master’s, I completed a Dual Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science and Mathematics at Université Paris-Saclay (2022-2025). The double programme gave me a solid foundation across probability, optimisation, machine learning theory and the practical side of software systems.

My research interests gravitate around fairness in AI, studying how machine learning models encode and amplify social biases and developing techniques to mitigate them. I’ve applied this to medical imaging, vision-language models and tabular data. More broadly, I enjoy problems at the intersection of mathematics and real life impact.

Outside research, I am a film enthusiast (see below), a long-distance runner and I care a lot about student life and community, having been president of a student association and co-founder of a competitive programming club.
